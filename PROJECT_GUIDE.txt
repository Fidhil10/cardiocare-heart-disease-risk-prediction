PROJECT OVERVIEW
================================================================================
CardioCare is a machine learning web application for early cardiovascular risk
detection. It combines:
- FastAPI backend (REST API on port 8001)
- Streamlit frontend (Web UI on port 8502)
- Logistic Regression model for risk classification

Purpose: Help clinicians identify patients at high risk of heart disease using
patient vitals and medical indicators.

================================================================================
TOPIC EXPLANATION: Heart Disease Prediction
================================================================================

What is Heart Disease?
- Leading cause of death globally
- Includes various conditions: coronary artery disease, arrhythmias, etc.
- Early detection allows for preventive intervention

Why Machine Learning?
- Learns patterns from historical patient data
- Identifies complex relationships between risk factors
- Provides consistent, evidence-based predictions
- Augments medical expertise with data-driven insights

The Model: Logistic Regression
- Binary classifier: outputs 0 (low risk) or 1 (high risk)
- Analyzes 11 medical features simultaneously
- Trained on cardiovascular disease datasets

Key Medical Parameters:
1. Age - Risk increases with age
2. Sex - Biological sex affects disease presentation
3. Chest Pain Type - Diagnostic indicator (TA, ATA, NAP, ASY)
4. Resting BP - Blood pressure; hypertension is risk factor
5. Cholesterol - High cholesterol increases risk
6. Fasting Blood Sugar - Indicator of diabetes
7. Resting ECG - Electrocardiogram at rest (Normal, ST, LVH)
8. Max Heart Rate - Lower rate = higher risk
9. Exercise Angina - Chest pain during exercise (Y/N)
10. Oldpeak - ST depression on ECG (heart stress indicator)
11. ST Slope - ECG segment pattern (Up, Flat, Down)

Clinical Value:
- Screening tool to identify patients for further investigation
- Risk stratification for resource allocation
- Population analytics to spot disease trends

EXAMPLE SCENARIO 1: LOW-RISK PATIENT
================================================================================

Patient Profile: John Smith
Age: 35, Sex: Male
Symptoms: Asymptomatic (no chest pain)

Vital Signs & Measurements:
  Chest Pain Type: ASY (Asymptomatic)
  Resting BP: 110 mm Hg (Normal)
  Cholesterol: 180 mg/dl (Normal)
  Fasting BS: 0 (Normal blood sugar)
  Resting ECG: Normal
  Max HR: 180 (Good heart rate response)
  Exercise Angina: N (No pain during exercise)
  Oldpeak: 0.0 (No ST depression)
  ST Slope: Up (Normal pattern)

MODEL PREDICTION: ✅ LOW RISK

Analysis:
- Young age (35) is protective factor
- No symptoms or risk indicators
- Normal blood pressure, cholesterol, heart rate
- Strong cardiovascular response to exercise
- ECG completely normal
- Recommendation: Maintain healthy lifestyle, routine check-ups

================================================================================
EXAMPLE SCENARIO 2: HIGH-RISK PATIENT
================================================================================

Patient Profile: Maria Garcia
Age: 68, Sex: Female
Symptoms: Chest pain with exertion

Vital Signs & Measurements:
  Chest Pain Type: TA (Typical Angina - classic warning sign)
  Resting BP: 150 mm Hg (HIGH - Stage 2 Hypertension)
  Cholesterol: 350 mg/dl (HIGH - major risk factor)
  Fasting BS: 1 (Elevated > 120 - diabetes indicator)
  Resting ECG: LVH (Left Ventricular Hypertrophy - heart strain)
  Max HR: 95 (LOW for age - poor exercise capacity)
  Exercise Angina: Y (YES - chest pain during exercise)
  Oldpeak: 3.5 (SIGNIFICANT ST depression - stress evidence)
  ST Slope: Down (CONCERNING ECG pattern)

MODEL PREDICTION: ⚠️ HIGH RISK

Analysis:
- Multiple cardiovascular risk factors present
- Senior age (68) + comorbidities (hypertension, diabetes)
- Classic angina symptoms during exercise
- Abnormal ECG showing heart stress
- Low exercise tolerance
- Recommendation: URGENT cardiology referral, medication, lifestyle changes,
  possible invasive testing (catheterization, stress test)

REAL-WORLD USAGE WORKFLOW
================================================================================

WEEK 1 - Initial Screening:
1. Patient comes to clinic with chest pain concern
2. Nurse measures vitals and enters data into CardioCare
3. Model processes data → HIGH RISK prediction
4. Doctor reviews results and patient history
5. Immediate cardiology referral ordered
6. Patient started on aspirin + preventive medication

MONTH 1 - Intervention:
1. Cardiology specialist performs stress test (confirms CAD)
2. Angioplasty procedure performed to open blocked artery
3. Patient discharged with cardiac rehab program

MONTH 6 - Follow-up:
1. Patient returns to clinic with new assessment
2. Lifestyle improvements: lost 15 lbs, exercises regularly, reduced stress
3. New vitals entered into CardioCare
4. Model predicts: LOW RISK
5. Dashboard shows positive progression

HOW TO USE THE APPLICATION
================================================================================

STARTING THE APP:
  1. Open PowerShell in project directory
  2. Run: python run_app.py
  3. Backend starts on http://localhost:8001
  4. Frontend starts on http://localhost:8502
  5. Open browser → http://localhost:8502

TAB 1: NEW PREDICTION
  1. Enter patient name
  2. Fill in all vital measurements
  3. Click "Analyze Risk Profile"
  4. View prediction result (High Risk / Low Risk)

TAB 2: PATIENT HISTORY & ANALYTICS
  1. View summary statistics
  2. See age distribution chart
  3. Analyze cholesterol patterns
  4. Browse complete patient registry

DATA PERSISTENCE:
  All predictions are stored in the backend database
  Historical data builds analytics over time
  Population insights improve with more patient data

TECHNICAL STACK
================================================================================
- Backend: FastAPI (Python REST framework)
- Frontend: Streamlit (Python web UI framework)
- Model: Scikit-learn Logistic Regression
- Database: SQLite (backend storage)
- Charts: Plotly (interactive visualizations)
- Environment: Python 3.13 in venv_new virtual environment

FILES:
- frontend/app.py → Streamlit UI code
- backend/main.py → FastAPI endpoints
- backend/models.py → ML model loading and prediction
- backend/schemas.py → Data validation schemas
- backend/database.py → Database operations
- run_app.py → Application launcher
